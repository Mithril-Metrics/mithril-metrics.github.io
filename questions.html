Suggest a question

We take short, specific questions on agri-food, water, climate and rural policy, for example “Do El Niño years cut rice output in Southeast Asia more than an average bad year?” We read every question; those that public data can answer well are listed above and may become one of our next case studies, tagged Reader questions.

We don’t reply individually, and we don’t collect contact details. To see every new case study, including any answer to your question, follow Mithril Metrics on LinkedIn and turn on notifications, or subscribe to our RSS feed.
